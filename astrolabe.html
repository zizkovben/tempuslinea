What am I looking at?

The Astrolabe is an instrument for finding what existed at a specific moment in time. Dial in an era on the outer ring, scrub to a year on the middle ring, and the inner ring shows you who was there — tied to the real astrological ages of the Celestial Layer.

It's not a full history browser — that's what the Timeline is for. This is for feeling time as one continuous gesture, the way an astronomer once dialed a date against the sky.

OUTER RING — drag or click to choose an epoch  ·  MIDDLE RING — drag to scrub through the epoch's years  ·  INNER RING — click a civilization to select it  ·  CENTER — click the selected civilization to view details  ·  PINCH OR SCROLL TO ZOOM
